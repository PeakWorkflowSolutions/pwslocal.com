    Our story

    Rooted here since 1999

    Long before espresso machines and the rich aroma of freshly ground beans filled the air, the patch of land known as Outback Espresso was once surrounded by orchards and a small strip of businesses, by Reva's hair salon and the diner next to the old apple packing plant.

    In 1999, the Grimes family started Outback Espresso with a simple vision: serve great coffee and treat every customer like a friend. In 2011, our second location, Outback North, was added to boost support for West Valley High School.

    Our roots remain the same. Every cup is grounded in the values our great-grandfather lived by: hard work, honesty, and community. When you drive up to order your favorite drink, you're helping to support our community, one cup at a time.
