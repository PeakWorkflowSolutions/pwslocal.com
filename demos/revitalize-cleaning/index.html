Our story

Built brick by brick

Revitalize started with Mari. After losing her job a few years ago, she began cleaning at a local church, then cleaning listings for a Yakima real estate team. With their encouragement, she turned it into her own business.

About a year later, the business had grown enough for her husband Damian to leave his long-time construction career and build it alongside her. Today they clean homes and businesses across Yakima, together.
